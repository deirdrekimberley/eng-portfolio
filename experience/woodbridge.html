Overview

At Woodbridge Group, I supported the testing and validation of automotive foam materials used in applications including vehicle seating, headrests, and interior components.

As my first engineering internship, the role introduced me to professional laboratory processes, standardized testing, engineering documentation, and working within an engineering organization. I learned to operate laboratory equipment, execute multiple types of material tests, collect and interpret test data, and communicate results through standardized reports.

As I became more familiar with the lab, I also began improving how I planned and reported testing, allowing me to run multiple validation activities in parallel and automate repetitive data-processing tasks.

01 Material Testing & Validation

Building a foundation in automotive material characterization and engineering test methods.

I performed mechanical, environmental, and durability testing on automotive foam materials used in products such as vehicle seats, headrests, and dashboards / interior components.

Testing Process

The role required me to learn how to prepare samples, operate laboratory equipment, follow defined test procedures, collect results, and document testing consistently.

Working across different test methods gave me an understanding of how engineering validation depends on repeatable procedures and reliable data, not simply obtaining a single measurement.

02 Lab Execution & Test Planning

Coordinating equipment, samples, and test duration to improve laboratory throughput.

Many validation tests had different preparation requirements, equipment needs, and durations. Rather than running each activity sequentially, I coordinated multiple tests in parallel, allowing longer-duration tests to continue while I prepared samples, operated other equipment, or processed results.

By planning around equipment availability and test duration, I was able to complete testing schedules that could otherwise span weeks within a matter of days.

03 Data & Reporting Automation

Reducing repetitive work between laboratory testing and engineering reports.

Test equipment produced machine results that ultimately needed to be transferred into standardized Excel-based test reports.

I used Excel and VBA macros to automate portions of this workflow, reducing repetitive manual data handling and making it faster to convert machine test results into usable reports.
