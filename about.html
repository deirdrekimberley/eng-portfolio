About Me

I’m a third-year mechanical engineering student at University of Waterloo with experience in mechanical design, manufacturing optimization, validation testing, and project management. I enjoy solving complex engineering problems and thrive in fast-paced, high-stakes environments where thoughtful design and rapid iteration are critical. My interests include biomechanical systems, robotics engineering, and product development, with a strong focus on creating solutions that can positively impact people’s lives. I’m driven by the belief that engineering should contribute to building a better and more accessible future. Outside of engineering, you can find me at a rink playing hockey, exploring the world, or trying something new. As someone who believes there is no limit to what you can learn, I strive to take every oppurtunity to expand my knowledge on really anything at anytime. I'm also always open to chat about engineering or non engineering related things feel free to reach out! The photos on this page pretty much sum up who I am outside of being an engineer but I think alot of those experiences have skills and lessons that I carry with me everyday that make me a better engineer.
